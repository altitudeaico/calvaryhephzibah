Worship Team Debrief

Pentecost Sunday

24 May 2026 · captured while it was still fresh
The main thing

Keeping the main thing the main thing.

The one thing we set out to do today — pull off a new set with a new band configuration, put together in a week, with no physical live rehearsal. Everything below is graded against that.

What went well

Wins we can be honest about.

The new set held together — and largely worked.

The bar we set was unfair to ourselves. Fresh arrangement, fresh band configuration, one virtual rehearsal call. And we still cleared it. Worth pausing on.

Abraham slotted in on drums.

Walking into a band you've never played with, with singers you've never met, and locking in — that takes real instinct. Abraham did that. The only player on the platform he'd shared a stage with before was Bolaji A. Welcome to the family.

Talkback / MD mic setup did its job.

The new in-ear / MD mic config gave the band a way to communicate without breaking the room. Worth keeping.

Uncle Caster led with conviction — including the spontaneous flow.

The set was tight, but Caster also created room for spontaneous worship and led it well. That's the harder thing to do — and he carried it.

Uniform held together.

Black and white across the platform. Small thing on paper, big thing in the room.

Opportunities to improve

What we can sharpen for next time.

Technical issues — symptom of crew availability earlier in the morning.

These keep re-surfacing. The root cause isn't gear; it's having the crew in the room early enough to catch them before service starts.

Audio levels — bass and guitar were quiet in the mix.

Hard to hear both on stage and (we suspect) front-of-house. Worth a level-pass at soundcheck rather than tweaking mid-service.

Esther's mic could have been louder.

Her parts were carrying the BVs across the set. The mix didn't lift her enough.

No click track or loops for the Praise set.

The praise medley would have held its tempo and transitions more cleanly with a click underneath. Worth introducing for medley sets going forward.

Thank you, family. We carried each other today — that was the whole point. God did the rest.
