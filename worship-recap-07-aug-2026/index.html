PART 1 How We Work

The group chat is not optional.

Before a single note The rehearsal opened here.

The rehearsal notice went out on Tuesday. Almost everybody had read it. Almost nobody had replied.

The explanation was fair enough: people were busy and meant to come back to it. But the effect is the same either way. When nobody answers, the person planning the service has no idea who is coming, who is covering which part, or whether anything needs changing before Thursday.

The standard we agreed is small. You do not need to write a paragraph. A thumbs up or a single emoji is enough. It takes two seconds and it tells the whole team you have seen it.

If something is a problem, that is exactly the message worth sending. Better to say it on Tuesday than to have it surface on Sunday morning.

PART 3 Worship Medley

Three songs, one journey.

Key of A♭ Most of the rehearsal went here. These three run together with no stop between them.

The shape is the same each time, and it is worth naming it plainly because it repeats: Dr Caster starts alone, the band comes in underneath, Esther and the rest join, then it builds. Nobody comes in at full volume. Everything grows.

Lord We Proclaim You Now — also known as "Release Your Power" or "Let Your Presence Fall"

01
Intro — Morayo plays the melody line to cue Dr Caster in
02
Verse one, solo — Dr Caster alone, band on hi-hat or ride only
03
Verse two — Esther and the band come in
04
Chorus — can run up to four times, depending on the room
05
Straight into Reign Jesus Reign — no stop

Per part. Morayo: chords through the verse. There is no keyboard, so the guitar is carrying it, and overdrive comes off for the verse because it does not suit the dynamic. Laolu: hi-hat or ride only under the solo verse — time, not fill. Dr Caster: verse one on your own, and wait for the melody cue.

Reign Jesus Reign — comes out of the chorus above with no break

01
Through once, solo — Dr Caster takes the whole song alone
02
Band enters softly and lifts gradually from there
03
The last two lines repeat — "King of Zion" and "Jesus reign"
04
Fourth time round the lyric changes — do not sing "and this is holy ground"

Per part. Everyone: the entry is soft, and the intensity is built rather than switched on. Dr Caster: which section loops, and how many times, is yours to set.

Bow Down and Worship — the transition into this one takes care, because the ear expects a different chord

01
Interlude — Morayo plays the melody line, not chords
02
Verse twice, solo — Dr Caster alone
03
Third time, band and Esther join
04
Fourth time to finish the verse section, then into the chorus

Per part. Morayo: melody line for the interlude. Chords were tried and they muddied the transition, so the melody keeps time and gives Dr Caster a clean cue without the key shift confusing the entry. Everyone: four verses in total, two solo and two together. Count them.

BJ ran the structure back at the end by asking the team to say it out loud rather than assuming it had landed. A good habit, worth keeping
PART 4 Offering & Close

Two simple ones to finish.

Key of A♭ Both stay in A♭, so there is no key change to manage after worship.

08
Higher Higher (offering)
09
You Are Worthy (end of service)

Higher Higher is the "every day I live for Jesus" song from the medley we did recently. Straightforward, and everyone already has it.

You Are Worthy — "King of kings, Lord of lords, You are worthy." An old one that Calvary has sung for years. It needs no arrangement, only a clean start.

Keeping both in A♭ was a deliberate call. Praise sits in B and everything after it sits a semitone below, so the only move in the whole service is the one drop into worship.
