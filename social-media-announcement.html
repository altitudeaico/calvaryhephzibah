Sunday Announcement

Beyond These Four Walls

For the announcer. A gentle encouragement to read from the front, before the benediction.

Read this About 30 seconds

Every week, the gospel goes out well beyond these four walls. We share short clips from our services on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts. They reach people who may never walk through our doors, and every one of us can help them travel further.

So here's a gentle encouragement

  • Follow us, so our clips come up in your feed.
  • When one of our clips comes up, give it a like.
  • Leave a quick comment.
  • And tap share or repost. That's the button that carries our clips onto new feeds.

None of this takes more than a few seconds, but it carries the gospel to places we could never reach on our own.

If you're not on social media, that's completely fine. But if you are, you can help us take the word beyond these walls this week.
