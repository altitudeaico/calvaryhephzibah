## ⚠️ Publishing caveat — "You Are Kings"

Do not publish still 10 as a standalone "YOU ARE KINGS" / "WE ARE SMALL GODS" quote card.
Psalm 82:6 and John 10:34 carry genuine interpretive complexity — isolated, it can read as
teaching believers are literally divine, which is a stronger and different claim than Bishop
Henry's point about representing the King's rule. The carousel frame must establish that
context *before* the line lands, and the caption must locate it inside the wider
"representing God's Kingdom" point rather than making "small gods" the headline. Verify the
transcript immediately before/after this section before writing the final caption — context
matters more than usual here.
