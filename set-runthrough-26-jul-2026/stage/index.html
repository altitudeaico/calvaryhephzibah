1
Intro
Backing track runs in. Everyone comes in together on the count. This entry has to be tight or the whole opener sits wrong.
2
We Lift Our Hands
Straight through. The last line turns into the lead-in for the next section rather than closing out.
3
Yes Lord
Comes straight off that lead-in. Lands on the amen.
4
Blessing and Honour
Into the every-tongue section. Keep the delivery staccato and on the original rhythm. Do not stretch the notes out, it pulls against the track.
5
Glory be to the Lord
Call and response, twice round. Kept deliberately simple so the worship leader can land it.
6
Higher, Every Day
Once through. Singing the phrase twice counts as once. Nothing added on top.
7
My Hands, My Feet
Straight in off Higher. There is no gap here.
8
Break
The gap inside the song, doubled to give it a breather. The song breaks down, it does not stop.
9
My Hands, My Feet
Back in and repeat to the end.
10
Outro
Land it together.
