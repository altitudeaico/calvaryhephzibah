## Opening prayer

Holy Spirit, we come. Today we look up to You and You alone. That You might open our
understanding. That You might reveal some new truth to us today, into our spirit man, that
will energise us, that will empower us to live victoriously. Take my mind, my heart, my
mouth, Lord, that You might use it to Your glory. That I might say only those things that
You want me to say today. In Jesus' precious name. Amen.

## The victory is already won

I know we have been looking at the series on altars, and I know that last week you had the
teaching on entering into the kingdom, and I was really so blessed listening to that. Today
we are going to be looking at altars and the believer's victory.

We have looked at what an altar is. And we also established that there are two kingdoms, and
those two kingdoms are at war with one another.

So the first thing I want to say today is this: that Jesus' victory brought defeat to the
kingdom of Satan. I know we know that, but it is good to remind ourselves over and over
again that Jesus has won the victory. When we talk about the believer's victory, it is not
that we are going to start fighting a new battle. We are fighting, but we are fighting from a
standpoint of victory. And it is very important that we go into any battle with the enemy
from that standpoint — that Jesus has already overcome.

What we are doing is to enforce that victory, that victory He has won for us. We are
enforcers, really. Enforcers of the victory that has been won by the cross.

Colossians 2:14–15 says: *He cancelled the record of charges against us and took it away,
nailing it to the cross. In this way, He disarmed the spiritual rulers and authorities. He
shamed them publicly by His victory over them on the cross.*

He disarmed them. Can you imagine an opponent, an enemy, that is armed, and his weapons have
been taken away from him? He disarmed the spiritual rulers and authorities by His victory on
the cross. And that victory, He handed over to us.

Romans 8:36–37 says: *As the Scripture says, "For Your sake we are killed every day; we are
being slaughtered like sheep."* So we might have many challenges, many tribulations. It says,
*despite all these things, overwhelming victory is ours through Christ Jesus.*

Overwhelming victory is ours through Christ Jesus. No matter what the enemy throws at us, no
matter the challenges of life, the ups and the downs — overwhelming victory is ours through
Christ Jesus.

So let us lift our heads high. No matter what the enemy tries to do to us, what he throws —
and he will, he will always come again and again — overwhelming victory is ours in Christ
Jesus. Amen.

## Point one: be strong in the Lord

The path to the believer's victory is outlined to us in Ephesians chapter 6. I am only going
to be able to handle a tiny bit of Ephesians chapter 6, because it is so packed and so rich.
But I think the little that we touch on today will bless us.

Verse 10: *A final word: be strong in the Lord and in His power.*

What comes to me in that statement is this: I can only be strong when I am in the Lord. That
is the only way I can be strong, especially against the enemy. Why? Because He, Jesus, is
stronger than the enemy.

When some children are bullying a child at school, and it happens that the dad is with him on
that day — what do you think the child would do? Because he knows his dad is stronger than
all the children bullying him. He too will do *shakara* to the bullies, isn't it? And say,
okay, come and touch me now. Because I know who is with me.

And so, it is only in the Lord that we can be strong. That is why Jesus says we must remain in
Him. The Bible says in James chapter 4: *submit yourself to God* — that is, yield to God's
authority — *and then you can resist the devil, and he will flee from you.*

So how do we submit? How do we remain in Christ? We submit ourselves to God through
obedience. Like I referred to in John chapter 15, that is where He said you must remain.
Remain means don't go anywhere else. Remain in Christ.

No matter the challenges — sometimes when we have challenges, some people will suggest, oh, I
know this man who can do this for you, I know this place where you can go for help. Remain in
Christ. Let us not seek help from strange altars. Remain in Christ, and He will grant us
overwhelming victory.

The other reason we need to remain is this: it is only those who are under authority who can
exercise authority. Remember the centurion in the Bible, who came to meet Jesus and said, *I
too am a man under authority. I say to this one, go, and he goes; and to that one, come, and
he comes.* What he is saying is that because he was under authority, he was able to exercise
authority.

So it is as long as we remain in Christ, as long as we are submitted to Him, that we can
exercise authority.

## Point two: put on the whole armour

The second point I want to take out of that Ephesians chapter 6 passage: it says *put on the
whole armour of God.* The whole. I want to put emphasis on the whole. Not just a part, not
just some of it. He says put on the whole armour *that you may be able to*. So that is a
condition. It is conditional, that we wear the whole armour.

And there are seven in that passage. The belt of truth. The body armour, or breastplate, of
righteousness. The shoes of peace that come from the good news and proclaiming the good news.
The shield of faith, to stop firing arrows. The helmet of salvation, to protect the head and
the mind. The sword of the Spirit, which is the Word of God. And praying in the Spirit at all
times.

The whole armour. I am going to consider only two of the seven today.

### The belt of truth

Jesus Himself declared: *I am the way, the truth and the life. No one can come to the Father
except through Me.*

The word "truth" in Greek is *aletheia*, which means uncovered, brought to light. So truth is
a person. Jesus says He is the truth.

The Bible also tells us in John 8:31–32: *you will know the truth, and the truth will set you
free.* So it is the truth that we know that sets us free. And we know that Jesus is the Word,
John tells us; Jesus is the truth, and the Word is the truth. They are all interconnected.

But also, truth must be upon our lips. Let us live in truth. As believers, we must not peddle
lies. The Bible says don't lie to one another, but let us tell the truth to one another. That
also matters. It is part of our armour.

Some people say some lies are white lies, isn't it? I don't believe there are any white lies.
I don't think there is any black lie. A lie is a lie. Let's remember, it is part of our
armour. Because there is no lie in Christ.

So when we are tempted to lie, let us remember that it is like taking off your armour. When a
soldier on the battlefield takes off his armour, what is that? He is exposing himself to
danger.

### The shield of faith

The Bible tells us what faith is. It says it is the evidence of things not seen.

Now let me just say this: faith does not deny the reality of what can be seen. It doesn't.
That is not faith. That is denial. Faith is not the same as denial. It does not deny reality.
But faith recognises that there is a higher truth than the truth that we can see. That is
what faith does.

Let me give an example. Think of a giant jumbo jet. If you want to travel to Nigeria, or you
want to go to America, you go on an aeroplane, and sometimes they are very massive. The first
time I went on a 747 I thought, my goodness, is this going to stay in the air? Because it was
so massive, and there were so many people, with all their luggage.

Now, I am a physicist, so I think about physics, I think about science. When you think about
a jumbo jet — I googled it, and they say it could have a mass of up to 570,000 kilograms.
With that mass you apply the acceleration due to gravity, times it by approximately 10 —
that is over 5 million Newtons. That is the force that, each time it goes up, pulls it down.
Five million Newtons.

So why does it lift up? Well, we know that it lifts up, doesn't it? And it stays up for
thousands of miles, from Dubai to Australia. It stays up in the air. Why? Because there is a
higher force that overcomes the force due to gravity. That force is called lift. That lift is
what lifts up the aeroplane.

And as it lifts up, the gravitational force is still acting. It never stops. But the higher
force lifts it up, and keeps it up.

So when we have situations in our lives and it looks impossible, by the reality that we know
of what we can see and what we can feel and what we know — let us remember that there is a
higher truth. A higher force that can overcome and overpower.

That first force will still be true. It will still be true. I think of this: the same river
Nile, where many of the Jewish children perished — many children were thrown into the river,
they were killed in the river. But that same Nile was where Moses was saved.

So because we know that something is true does not mean that a bigger truth does not exist.
That is what faith does. When we lift up the shield of faith, we remind ourselves that
although we are conscious of, we acknowledge, the truth of the reality around us, God is
higher. And He can bring in a higher reality, a higher truth, above what we can see.

## Point three: the strategies of the devil

Verse 11 of Ephesians 6 says: *put on the whole armour, so that you may be able to stand firm
against all the strategies of the devil.*

So I want us to look at what the strategies are. I am not going to be able to outline all of
them, but I have about four strategies of the devil that I want us to look at quickly.

### One: the influence of evil altars

If we look at Acts chapter 19, Paul was in Ephesus, and he had persuaded many people about the
Lord Jesus Christ. He told them the gospel, and many came, and they brought all their
different artefacts, all the different books of magic and sorcery, and they burnt them. He
told them the handmade gods were not gods at all.

And so we see one of the craftsmen saying: of course, I am not just talking about the loss of
public respect of our business, I am also concerned that the temple of the great goddess
Artemis will lose its influence. The influence. The influence of evil altars.

How do we recognise them in our days? Sometimes we find an unfortunate repeating pattern
happening in our lives — in a person, or in a family. Maybe a pattern of someone falling ill
whenever they need to sit an important exam. Just the day before, they will fall ill. Or you
see a pattern of someone missing a critical cut-off point, maybe just one mark below the pass
mark. In some places you might see that it is difficult for the girls to get married. It is a
pattern.

Anywhere we see those kinds of unfortunate patterns, we need to start to see that it might be
the influence of an evil altar. I am sure we are going to have a teaching sometime later down
the line about how to deal with evil altars. My role today is just to identify them, and not
how to deal with them.

### Two: fear

The Bible says we are not ignorant of the strategies. We mustn't be. Fear is a strategy of the
devil.

The acronym FEAR, people say, is "false evidence appearing real." And fear can be so real. It
is a thought in your head, in your mind, that something is true, and yet it is totally not
true. And you see the evidence, and you think, oh, that is the evidence, I can see it, I can
feel it — and yet it is totally untrue.

And fear can drive us to do many things, to do things which we would normally not do. That is
why we need to know: when the enemy wants to bring fear into our lives, we cut it off
immediately, so that it doesn't cause us to do things which we don't want to do.

### Three: deception

Jesus Himself warned His disciples, and warned us, in Matthew 24. He says take heed that you
be not deceived.

Deception makes wrong actions look good and acceptable. And in these last days we must
particularly beware of false teachings, false prophets who come disguised as harmless sheep
but are really vicious wolves.

The Lord opened my eyes recently to this passage of Scripture in Matthew chapter 4, when the
devil was tempting Jesus. I never noticed this before until recently. The Bible says the devil
took Him to the holy city, to the highest point, and said, *if You are the Son of God, jump
off. For the Scripture says He will order His angels to protect You, and they will hold You up
with their hands, so You won't even hurt Your foot on a stone.*

That is the devil quoting the Scriptures. Because in the previous temptation, when he said
Jesus should command the stones to become bread, Jesus had said you must only live by the Word
of God. So he said, okay, if that is what you want, I know the Scriptures. So he quoted the
Scriptures.

And then Jesus responded and said, *the Scripture also says.* And the Holy Spirit highlighted
for me that word — *also*. That gave me the understanding. It says, the Scripture *also* says,
*you must not test the Lord your God.*

The devil quoted Psalm 91:11–12. And Jesus quoted Deuteronomy 6:16 — a separate verse of
Scripture. But Jesus said, the Scripture also says. And I noticed that the devil didn't argue
with Jesus. Because he knew the meaning.

So Jesus is saying: if the Scripture says this — yes, it is true, Psalm 91:11–12 says this —
but the Scripture also says that. So you must interpret this in the light of that.

Because some people will just go down one street, and they just say, the Scripture says this,
and that's it. It is just a one-way street. But if the Scripture also says this, we must
interpret one in the light of the other. And I found it intriguing that the devil did not
argue with Jesus. He actually knew that that was the right thing to have done. So that was
why it was a temptation.

### Four: distraction, division and discouragement

I have lumped three of them together.

Distraction will overwhelm people with busyness, worldly worries, minor issues, so that they
neglect spiritual habits like prayer. It is a strategy of the devil.

Division — fostering anger, pride, suspicion, unforgiveness among brethren. It is a strategy
of the devil. And how do we overcome that? One of the things God has helped me to see, I
think it is in Colossians 3:13, He says *make allowance for one another.*

I always say that to myself. Whenever I think somebody has done something to hurt me, I remind
myself: let me make allowance. I start to make excuses. Maybe they forgot. Maybe something
happened. Maybe. And I find it very helpful for me personally. It helps me not to keep
grudges. It helps me not to be angry unnecessarily, because I take that Colossians 3:13 to
heart. Let me make allowance, let me make maybe. And that can help us to overcome the
strategy of the devil.

And then another D word: discouragement. The enemy constantly reminds us about our failures in
the past, to induce guilt and make us feel unworthy of God's grace and God's forgiveness.

So let us summarise the strategies of the devil: the influence of evil altars; number two,
fear — false evidence appearing real; number three, deception; and then distractions,
divisions and discouragement.

## Point four: there is an evil day

Another point I want us to notice in that Ephesians passage is that there is an evil day.
There is an evil day when we need to stand and resist the enemy.

Ephesians 6:13 says: *Therefore, put on every piece of God's armour, so that you will be able
to resist the enemy in the time of evil.* The King James Version says *in the evil day.* Then,
after the battle, you will still be standing.

There is an evil day. And the evil day comes in different forms and guises for different ones
of us. But the Bible says when the evil day comes, we can still remain standing. We can stand,
and remain standing.

So what does the evil day look like? I am thinking of that woman who provided a room for
Elisha. The Bible says Elisha prayed for her, and she got pregnant, and she had a son. And
then what happened to the son? One day, when her child was older, he went out with his father,
who was working with the harvesters. Suddenly he cried out, my head hurts, my head hurts. His
father said to one of his servants, carry him home to his mother. So the servant took him
home, and his mother held him on her lap, but around noontime he died. She carried him up and
laid him on the bed of the man of God, then shut the door and left him there.

That was an evil day. An evil day. A day that started like any ordinary day.

But we know the rest of that story, and we know that it ended in victory for that woman. It
ended in victory.

## How we enforce the victory

When the evil day comes, let us remember what we need to do. We are to enforce Jesus' victory
in the evil day. The Bible says *resist the devil and he will flee from you.* So how do we
resist the devil?

**First, it starts with submitting yourself to God.** I have said it before and I am going to
repeat it: it is a person under authority who can exercise authority.

Do you remember the seven sons of Sceva? They were trying to cast out a demon, and they said,
in the name of Jesus of Nazareth whom Paul preaches, I cast you out. They were not under
authority, so they could not exercise authority over the demonic world. They couldn't. And the
Bible says the demons ran after them, took off their clothes and beat them. He couldn't
exercise authority. It is only a person under authority who can exercise authority.

**Second, praying in the Spirit.** In that Ephesians 6 it talks about praying in the Spirit.
The Bible says in Romans 8:26–27 that we do not know how we ought to pray, but the Spirit
Himself intercedes for us through wordless groans. That is one of our weapons as people of
God. Let us use it. Let us use it with great effect.

**Third, resist using the Word of God.** It is the sword of the Spirit. That is how Paul wrote
it in Ephesians: the sword of the Spirit, which is the Word of God.

I am going to list a few things we do with the Word of God. We meditate on it — Joshua 1:8,
*study this book continually, meditate on it day and night.* We never plan for the evil day,
but it comes. Let us already be armed. Armed with the Word of God.

Second, we believe the Word of God. And thirdly, we declare the Word of God. The Bible says
*they have defeated him by the blood of the Lamb and by the word of their testimony* —
Revelation 12:11. We defeat the devil by the word of our testimony. That is the declaration of
our lips. The Word of God, we declare it with power, in faith.

The Bible says when we resist the devil, what does he do? He flees. So we keep resisting him
until he flees.

## "Whatever you do not resist, you are permitting"

I remember, I think it was autumn 2019, and I have given this testimony before. I took the flu
jab that autumn. And then I had the flu.

Three consecutive times, almost two weeks each. Normally when I have the flu it would last
maybe five days, and then I would get better. I would take Lemsip and I would get better and
get stronger within five days. This particular one lasted two weeks, and I took everything,
and it just persisted.

At that time my job was as a supply teacher in schools, so you only get paid for when you
work. Two weeks in November. Two weeks in December, I had another bout. And then came
February, and I had another bout of the same thing.

And I started to ask myself — biology teaches us that when you have an infection once, your
body gets immunity to that particular infection. So I was a little bit confused, but a bit
angry as well, because it meant that I was not able to earn any money. November, December,
January, and now February.

And I was getting quite worried. So I started to pray. I asked my husband. He laid hands on
me. He prayed for me. I didn't get better. It was getting worse. On the Friday I could feel
it — you know, it starts in the throat, and then on the Saturday the body weakness, and it was
just getting worse. And at that time they had just given me an assignment in a school for
three weeks, starting the Monday. So I was thinking, shall I phone them and cancel?

But on the Sunday, as I woke up — usually the Holy Spirit speaks to me particularly when I am
rising up from sleep. And the Holy Spirit said: *whatsoever you permit on earth is permitted
in heaven.*

That was the Scripture I woke up with. And I thought to myself: I didn't permit this flu. I
didn't permit it. The evidence is that I asked my husband to pray against it, and he prayed. I
didn't permit it.

And then the Spirit of God said, James 4:7 — *resist the devil and he will flee from you.* He
said: whatsoever you do not resist, you are permitting.

That is what He taught me that morning. He said, whatsoever I am not resisting, I am
permitting it.

And so, with that revelation — Jesus said, I will build My church, when Peter said, You are
the Son of God. He said, upon this, upon revelation. He said flesh and blood has not revealed
it to you. Upon revelation. Revelation is very important in the church, because that gives us
victory.

Upon that revelation I stood up. And I then knew how to get my healing. I started to say: this
flu in my body, I resist you in the name of Jesus. I do not permit you to stay in my body. I
resist you.

And I resisted the enemy, and I went to sleep. I had breakfast, and I went to sleep. When I
woke up, I could feel something lift off my body, and I was healed. I went downstairs and I
prepared lunch, and that was it.

But I learned a lesson about resisting the enemy. The Bible says whatsoever we permit on earth
is permitted in heaven. But whatever we disallow on earth is disallowed in heaven.

**And the last thing we need to do to resist the devil is to persist.** Persistence. The Bible
says, Galatians 6:9, *let us not get tired of doing what is good. At the right time we will
reap a harvest of blessing, if we don't give up.*

If we don't give up. So we persist.

So the summary of how to enforce the victory: resist the devil and he will flee. How?
Submitting to God, praying in the Spirit, using the Word of God, and persisting in prayer,
declaring the Word of God.

## Summary

Only in the Lord can we have victory over our enemy. Only as we remain in Him — in our hearts,
not just physically. Somebody can come to church and do all the right things, but the heart is
far away from Him. It is in our heart we must remain, and not wander.

We must put on the whole armour of God for it to be effective, not just part of it.

We must be conscious of the strategies of the devil, which are the influence of evil altars;
fear, false evidence appearing real; deception; and distractions, divisions and
discouragement.

There is usually an evil day. When that evil day comes, we need to stand and to resist the
adversary. And resist means refuse to give in.

We need to pray in the Spirit, use the Word of God, which is the sword of the Spirit. We
meditate, we believe, and we declare it in faith. And we persist until the enemy flees and
victory is won.

Sometimes it is good, or needed, for us to involve others in our standing against the enemy.
Sometimes let us not stand alone. You remember Jesus took the three close disciples with Him
in Gethsemane? He didn't go alone. He recognised that He needed that support, and so do we. So
sometimes let us not do it alone. And sometimes it might even involve the whole church that
needs to stand with us until victory is won.

## Action calls

When we hear the Word of God, it is important for us to respond, so that it can impact our
lives.

I don't know if someone is here who has never given their heart to Jesus. Today, the Bible
says, today is the accepted time. Today is the day of salvation. Because it is only when we
are found in Him that we can be strong against the enemy.

I don't know if there is anyone who may need to rededicate their life to Christ. Because it is
only as we remain in Him that we can be strong against the enemy. If you are in those classes,
can I encourage you to ask Jesus into your life? Ask Him to come and be your Lord and your
Saviour, and He will come. He has promised that He will, and He will do just that, and your
life will not remain the same.

Do you need to study the Word of God more, meditating on it day and night?

Are there altar images that you need to get rid of, that they might remove the influence of
those altars, because that is part of the strategies of the devil?

Are you going through an evil day at the moment? Remind yourself that Jesus has already won
the victory on your behalf. He said it is finished. Recognise that every handwriting that is
contrary and against you has been nailed to His cross. Yes, it has been nailed to His cross,
so you don't have to carry it. That is what you tell the devil: that every handwriting against
me has been nailed to His cross, so I don't have to carry this. It is already finished.

And then, maybe we all need to put on the whole armour — the belt of truth. Remember, we
mentioned truth. The world will tell us it doesn't matter, it is just a little lie to get us
out of an inconvenience. But it isn't. It is a believer taking off their armour.

And then, if you are in an evil day, remember that you might need to get someone else, or
other people, to stand with you.

## Closing prayer

I am going to pray for us using Hebrews 13:20–21. *Now may the God of peace, who brought up
from the dead our Lord Jesus, the great Shepherd of the sheep, and ratified an eternal
covenant with His blood — may He equip you with all you need for doing His will. May He
produce in you, through the power of Jesus Christ, every good thing that is pleasing to Him.
And all glory to Him forever and ever. Amen.*

Father, I just pray by Your Spirit that You water these words in our hearts, that it will
empower every single one of us to live in the victory that Jesus has won for us. In Jesus'
name. Amen.
