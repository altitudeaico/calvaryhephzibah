# Living Under The Rule Of The KING

**Rev Ifeayinchukwu Obi · Sunday 23 August 2026**
Entering, Experiencing and Manifesting The Kingdom Of GOD · John 3:3–7 (KJV)

Source: TurboScribe transcript, **first 30 minutes only** of a 41-minute sermon. Everything
below is from that window. The final ~11 minutes are not transcribed — see Gaps.

Third Sunday of Rev Obi's kingdom thread. He revises 9 August at length before starting,
so a lot of the first ten minutes is recap and should not be treated as new material.

---

## The spine

**Kingdom = king + domain.** A realm where the King is ruling. Not far away, not after you
die. "The kingdom of God is in you. The king is in your heart, waiting to rule over your life."

**You enter it by a new birth**, exactly as Jesus told Nicodemus. Born of water is the
physical birth; born again is the second one, into the spirit realm. That is why John 3:3–7
was the reading.

**The evidence you are born again is obedience**, and obedience is how the King actually
rules. His test: "If you hear his word and you don't obey it, he's not ruling. You are ruling."

**The pearl is not an abstraction.** He asks the question and answers it directly: the pearl
is already inside you. "The precious Holy Spirit, the King that is eternal, the King that is
immortal, the only true God is inside. Whether you believe it or not. You have found it."

**The turn, and the whole point of the sermon.** The two men in Matthew 13 found the thing
and responded with joy. We have found the same thing and our response does not match:

> "We appear to have found the kingdom, but our response does not support it. At best, our
> response is timid. At best, it is lukewarm. At best, it is civilised."

Not because we doubt it. "Because we don't know the value of what is inside us."

**Why Jesus taught in parables** — the answer he gives is unusual and very clippable. A
parable is a provocation. If it wakes a desire in you to know more, He comes running:

> "If it wakes up a desire, he runs to meet you immediately. When you set out to explore
> them, the Lord will meet with you immediately. And that's why he teaches in parables."

**Repentance is a change of direction, not a mood.** John the Baptist's "change your mind"
means change your perspective on life. "You cannot continue going the way you're going and
expect to be inside the kingdom."

## CARE FLAGS — read before clipping anything else

**1. The passage at 28:28–29:09. Do not clip. Do not paraphrase. Do not quote.**
Pastoral advice on a personal legal matter, given to a room he knows, and only safe with the
whole context around it. Out of the room it inverts, and it is the sort of subject where a
30-second clip can affect real people. Highest-risk passage in the sermon by some distance.
Leave it in the full recording. This note is deliberately non-specific: **this file is in a
public repo.**

**2. The word at 22:04–22:22.**
His own blunt phrasing for a person with no father to guide them, serving a theological point.
Fine in the room, wrong in a caption. Do not use, even quoted.

**3. His personal testimony (24:23–26:11).**
Turning down an executive directorship at a bank, being shown what waited down that road, and
having drawn no salary since 1998. Strong material and he tells it publicly and often. But it
includes "I would have died a despicable death," which needs the whole story around it or it
lands as a threat. Only clip with the resolution attached.

**4. "Leave those people in Africa that clap hands" (9:51).**
He is voicing an attitude he is criticising, not stating his own view. Guaranteed to be read
as his own if clipped. Do not isolate.

## Further carousel candidates

1. **Why Jesus taught in parables** — the "he runs to meet you immediately" answer. Probably
   the strongest single idea in the sermon and nothing else in the series covers it. Pairs
   with the `immediately` clip.
2. **Timid, lukewarm, civilised** — the diagnosis, standalone. Uncomfortable in the right way.
   Needs care so it reads as invitation and not as a telling-off.
3. **Obedience is how the King rules** — "if you hear his word and you don't obey it, he's not
   ruling, you are ruling." Concrete, and it gives a reader something to do.
4. **Your soul is worth more than the world** — Matthew 16:26 through his lens, and his
   application: hold that next to whatever you are facing and the circumstance shrinks.

Series continuity to keep straight: 9 Aug was the treasure in the field (Matthew 13:44), and
`treasure-in-a-field` already exists as a carousel. 23 Aug is the pearl (13:45–46). Rev Obi
explicitly links them — "the question we had last week for you was, look at the responses of
these people." The pearl carousel is the direct sequel and should be posted as one.
