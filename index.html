Welcome

Come as you are

You do not need to have it together to walk in. You do not need to know anyone, dress a certain way, or know when to stand up. Come as you are, and see for yourself.

You are welcome here A word of welcome from our church family

Calvary Hephzibah is a family church in the heart of Manchester. We have been here for over thirty years, and people from every kind of background call this place home.

Sunday mornings run for about two hours. There is worship, there is teaching from the Bible, and there is time to pray. Children are welcome in the room with us. Afterwards, stay for a drink and meet someone.
