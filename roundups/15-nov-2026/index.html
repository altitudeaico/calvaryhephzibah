Welcome · 15 November 2026

A note of thanks

Thank you,
Prof. B Adebisi.

Thank you for sharing Bodies as Service with Calvary Hephzibah. Your message reminded us that the gifts we already have can be offered in service to God.

We've put together a simple, guided look at the message and what we have prepared from it. You don't need to know anything about social media to enjoy it.

The message

What did we hear on Sunday?

Prof. B Adebisi's message was simple: “Let's not complicate it.” Here are three ideas he shared.

01

Offer what you already have.

Our abilities, gifts and opportunities can be used to serve God. Romans 12:1.

02

Show up and do your part.

Don't worry about everybody else's role. Begin with what you're good at.

03

Keep learning and practising.

Prof. B Adebisi encouraged us to grow our abilities through knowledge and practice.

This is a short introduction, not the entire sermon. You'll find the full recording later in this journey.
