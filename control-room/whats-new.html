What's New

The desk keeps getting easier to drive. Here's what changed — and what it means for you on a Sunday.

July 2026
Latest update

A big tidy-up of the operator desk, plus a clearer look on screen. Nothing you knew has moved far — most things are now quicker to reach.

🧭
Much easier to get around
The desk now opens straight onto the live set, with four clear buttons across the top — Bible Verses, Name Tags, Order of Service and Announcements. Tap one to jump right to what you need instead of scrolling through everything.
On a Sunday: tap a button to open its tools, do the thing, then tap Done to drop back to the live set. The Next / Blank controls stay put at the bottom the whole time.
📱
Run the whole thing from your phone — anywhere
The desk now works properly on a phone screen. Anyone on the team can drive the stream from their phone — even from another room, or from home if you're covering remotely.
Handy for: stepping away from the desk, covering a service when you can't be in the building, or a second person helping from their seat.
👁️
The Preview button is now green — and easy to find
The "see what's going out" button is green now, so it stands out at a glance, and it no longer sits on top of the Next button. Tap it any time to check exactly what the congregation and the stream are seeing.
💷
Giving details look clearer on screen
When you put the church bank details up, they now sit neatly in the top corner of the screen instead of taking over the whole picture — so worship can carry on behind them. Easier to read, and far less disruptive.
Where to find it: Announcements → the Giving card → Show on screen. Tap again to clear it.
✅
Midweek meeting days — corrected
We'd had the two meetings round the wrong way. It's now right: Prayer Meeting is Wednesday, 7:30pm, and Application of the Word (Bible Study) is Friday, 7:30pm — both online on Zoom, Meeting ID 330 030 8969.
To put one up: Announcements → tap Prayer Meeting or Bible Study → Show on screen. The details are already filled in.
📖
Scripture and lyrics are easier to read
Verses and song words now sit on a soft red Calvary-branded backdrop, so they stand out clearly over any camera shot — bright stage, dark room, busy background — and the whole overlay looks a little more "us."
🎯
You can nudge the on-screen text
If a verse or a lyric is sitting awkwardly — over someone's face, or too low — you can now move it and resize it live, and lyrics and scripture can each sit in their own spot.
Where: the settings (gear) → Position & size → tap Move & resize, drag it where you want, then lock it.
Thank you for serving on the desk — this is all to make your Sunday smoother.
Spotted something confusing, or got an idea to make it easier? Tell Bolaji — the desk keeps improving because the team says what's not working.
